# External custodians

Begin with the [Polish archive network](../countries/poland/README.md) and [National Archives of Finland](../countries/finland/README.md). The source directory and upstream references also provide routes for displacement, forced labour and emigrant records.

Follow actual custody rather than restricting research to modern borders. Do not assume that all records created in an imperial jurisdiction were transferred to a Russian archive. Individual collection and territorial coverage remain subject to review.
