# Russian Empire

Use event-date jurisdictions rather than today's borders. Start with the [National Library administrative reference](https://nlr.ru/res/refer/r_imp/) and dated settlement lists in [the Historical Library](https://elib.shpl.ru/). Component datasets have narrower coverage than their overall titles.

Gubernia, uyezd, volost and religious jurisdiction are separate lookup layers. Administrative changes, transferred fonds and later borders can divide related records among regional, central and foreign repositories. Consult the upstream ex-ussr-locale-guide and source-strategy references, then follow the [place workflow](finding-a-volost.md).

This directory does not claim an exhaustive imperial volost gazetteer.
