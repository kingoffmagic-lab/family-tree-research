# Project policy

This policy applies to this starter and cases initialized through it. It takes precedence over the bundled upstream instructions where they allow CAPTCHA attempts, sending correspondence, or optional uploads.

## Public research and human steps

Agents use free public sources and local materials supplied for the investigation. At login, CAPTCHA, payment, registration, or access restrictions: stop that route, record the general URL and required human action in the private case's plan/blocked.md, and continue other useful routes. Credentials, payment details, and session tokens stay out of logs and Git.

Institutional requests may be drafted locally with current official instructions. The user handles submission and replies. Agents do not send messages, submit forms, purchase access, register accounts, or upload family documents, photographs, or DNA data. Connecting a service does not change these defaults.

## Living people

Relevant historical and biographical research about living relatives is allowed. Keep it within the family-history question. Exclude current home addresses, personal contacts and social accounts, identifiers, finances, and health data. Explain sensitive findings neutrally and privately.

Only people explicitly recorded as deceased appear in default generated results. Private output is opt-in. Review free-text source titles, URLs, notes, and attachments before sharing any result. Automated filtering cannot establish consent or detect every reference to an unregistered person.

Use the bundled pinned dependency during research. Check or replace upstream only for an explicit maintenance request.

## Evidence and storage

Keep originals unchanged, register provenance, preserve uncertain readings, and separate namesakes. The upstream evidence protocol governs claims and source independence. A blocked search is not a no-match; no-match is not proof of absence.

Keep every real case outside the public toolkit. Toolkit contributions use general service URLs and fictional examples, never family scans, exact record links, saved searches, private case paths, or copied case narratives.

For institutional drafts and recipient selection, consult the bundled [correspondence reference](../vendor/ex-ussr-kinship-research/references/correspondence.md); this project policy still requires the user to submit requests.
