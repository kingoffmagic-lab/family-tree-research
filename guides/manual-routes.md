# Human access routes and institutional drafts

Find the institution's official access or request instructions. Record the general service URL, the collection sought, and exactly what the user must do. Keep paid, authenticated, reading-room, and request-based access separate from freely available scans.

Suggested request draft:

> I am researching a family-history question concerning [person], [event], approximately [year], in [historical locality and jurisdiction]. Could you identify whether relevant records are held by your institution and the applicable catalogue or access procedure? Known references: [references]. Please advise any eligibility, copying, or fee requirements before work begins.

This is a draft, not a submission. The user verifies current requirements and sends it. Attachments, identity documents, credentials, and payment data do not belong in toolkit examples. Treat any reply as a new source in the private case.
