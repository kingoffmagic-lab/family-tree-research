# Your first research session

1. Tell your agent which family question matters and what you already know. Approximate dates and places are useful; leave unknowns unknown.
2. Inventory documents, existing trees, photographs, letters, and earlier research. Ask relatives yourself; the interview questions below can help.
3. Ask the agent to initialize an external case using scripts/init_case.py. Put supplied files in originals/ without editing them.
4. Enter the initial people and competing candidates in the upstream registers. Family memory is a source with limits, not a documentary conclusion.
5. Choose one event: a birth, marriage, death, move, military service, or repression episode. Use the directory and historical place lookup to identify promising custodians.
6. Log each search and retain permitted evidence. At a barrier, record the human action and continue another route.
7. Review the evidence, run the audit, and record the next useful question. Build a report when it helps; missing branches need not delay it.

Useful interview questions: Which documents already exist? Who preserved an album? What names changed? What was the village called then? Are dates exact or remembered? Who supplied each story? Record the answer's provenance and uncertainty.

For scanning: preserve the original file, capture the whole page including margins and reverse, note the document's owner and context, and use working copies for rotation and crops. Do not upload it to a third-party OCR service through this workflow.

To resume: read case AGENTS.md, research-state.md, plan/BOARD.md, and the searches relevant to the current question. New searches need a new reason when repeating earlier work.
